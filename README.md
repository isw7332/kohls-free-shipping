## The full Kohl's shipping rate table (and the $49 vs. $75 confusion)

Kohl's own pages don't agree with each other right now, so both numbers deserve attention. The customer service documentation lists free standard shipping on orders of **$49 or more**, with an $8.95 standard fee below that. The free-shipping landing page, on the other hand, currently advertises free standard shipping with a **$75** purchase, "no exclusions, no promo codes" — and a deals guide updated in April 2026 verified the $49 threshold as still current, lining up with the help center chart.

Since Kohl's hasn't reconciled these, the safe play is simple: treat **$75** as your guaranteed target. If the $49 threshold is active on your order, you get free shipping early; if only the $75 rule applies, you're still covered. Either way you avoid an $8.95 surprise.

### The post-discount trap that kills free shipping

Kohl's calculates your order total **after all transaction discounts and promotions**. This is the single most common way shoppers talk themselves into free shipping and then get charged anyway.

Concrete example: your cart sits at $55, comfortably over the threshold. You apply a 15% off code and the total drops to $46.75 — under $49, and under $75 by a mile. Suddenly you're paying $8.95 to ship a discounted order. The fix is to check your post-discount total before assuming the shipping is free, or to prioritize a dollar-off threshold over stacking percentage codes when you're near the line.

## Four ways to get free Kohl's shipping without any code

Beyond the spending threshold, Kohl's has three other free-shipping routes, and one of them costs you nothing but a click.

1. **Free In-Store Pickup and free Ship to Store.** Order online, collect at the store, pay zero shipping. This works on any order total and sidesteps the threshold math entirely. If there's a Kohl's near you and you're not in a rush, it's the cleanest option on this list.
2. **Gift cards always ship free** by standard delivery, per both the shipping chart and the help center.
3. **MVC status on a Kohl's Card.** Spend $600 on the Kohl's Card in a calendar year (January 1 through December 31) and you become a Most Valued Customer. MVCs receive monthly free-shipping opportunities, and Kohl's has been running periods of free standard shipping with no minimum for Kohl's Charge MVCs — that promotion carries an end date (April 11 on the current page), so it's a window, not a permanent entitlement. Two fine-print items: you must be signed into your Kohls.com account linked to your Kohl's Rewards account, and surcharges on oversized items still apply. Worth noting the card program itself transitioned to Capital One in early 2025, so older guides describing the card terms may be out of date.
4. **The spending threshold itself** — $49 per the help center, $75 per the promo page — which you now know to calculate *after* discounts.

What none of these cover: Alaska, Hawaii, PO Boxes and APO/FPO military addresses are limited to standard ground (5–10 business days, no priority options), and items like pressurized spray cans, hairspray and nail polish can't travel by priority service at all due to air transport rules. Oversized or heavy items carry a surcharge that shows up on the product page — free shipping doesn't erase it.
